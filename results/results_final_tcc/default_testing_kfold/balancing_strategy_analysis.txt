## OVERALL CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. BINARY CLASSIFICATION:
   - Best strategy: TomekLinks (+0.0003, negligible improvement)
   - Worst strategy: SMOTETOMEK (-0.0312)
   - Baseline (none) outperforms most balancing strategies

2. MULTICLASS CLASSIFICATION:
   - Best strategy: TomekLinks (-0.0011, negligible degradation)
   - Worst strategy: SMOTEN (-0.0166)
   - Baseline (none) consistently outperforms all balancing strategies

3. GENERAL OBSERVATIONS:
   - TomekLinks is the most conservative strategy, showing minimal impact
     on performance in both scenarios
   - Oversampling strategies (ADASYN, SMOTEN, SMOTETOMEK, SMOTEENN) generally
     degrade performance more than undersampling (RandomUnderSampler)
   - For this specific gene classification task, the default (no balancing)
     approach appears optimal
   - The dataset may not have severe class imbalance issues that would benefit
     from rebalancing techniques, or the models are already handling the
     imbalance effectively

4. RECOMMENDATION:
   Use "none" (no balancing) as the default strategy for both binary and
   multiclass classification tasks in this gene classification project.
   If balancing is required, TomekLinks is the safest choice with minimal
   performance impact.
